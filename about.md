# Icons and Colors Unleashed

Use different icons and colors for each gamemode, even in dual mode!

Use the new icon in the garage to select different colors and enable / diable glow for every gamemode separately.

Clicking the selected icon allows you to change the icon for that player and gamemode.

You can even swap players icons and colors with just a click. 

All colors are unlocked (even when you do not have them). Use the settings to enable all icons if you want to try them out!

Big inspiration on [separate-dual-icons](https://github.com/Weebifying/separate-dual-icons-geode) and [custom-gamemode-colors](https://github.com/marlenemeows/custom-gamemode-colors).
